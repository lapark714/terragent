# Terragent: how it works (60 s)

Source brief: `../terragent-remotion-prompt.md`. Facts: `facts.json`. Timing: `src/timing.json`, generated by `scripts/score.py`.

## Concept

Three directions were considered.

1. **One object, one job.** A single pin carries a single question through the whole company. Every agent is a stop on its route, and the montage is the same pin landing all over the planet.
2. **Mission-control wall.** A fixed instrument wall where panels light up in turn. Clear, but static, and it reads as a dashboard.
3. **Orbital descent.** The film is one unbroken fall from orbit to ground. Beautiful, but it can't carry a business process.

**Chosen: 1.** It is the brief's carrying object, and it makes "no human touched this" visible, because the pin never leaves the frame and nobody else ever handles it.

Signature moments:
- The camera falls through the pin into a flooded town.
- A draft is thrown back, and the pin is flung home along a red arc.
- Twelve agents circle the pin while the human counter holds at zero.

## Decisions (◆)

- ◆ **Repo location.** The brief says `~/Code/terragent`; the repo is at `/Users/laurence/terragent`. All film work is in `video/`, and the rest of the repo is untouched apart from excluding `video` from the app's `tsconfig.json` and ESLint config.
- ◆ **Logo files.** The `~/Downloads` PNGs are absent. Used `src/components/site/brand/{mark,lockup}.png`, the founders' artwork as alpha masks. Not redrawn or traced.
- ◆ **Music is synthesized.** No music-generation key is available. The score is composed in `scripts/score.py` with numpy. Timing is authored rather than beat-detected, so audio and picture share one table.
- ◆ **Sign-off gets 3 s, not 2.** Its two lines need about 3 s at 0.3 s per word. The montage gave back that second.
- ◆ **Mission counters.** "Requests answered / emails sent / quotes paid" would be invented usage figures, which the brief's Never list bans. The counters count this film's one job instead: steps run by agents, agents working, human interventions.
- ◆ **Single global pin and globe.** One layer each, above and below all scenes, keyframed in `src/choreography.ts`. Handoffs can't drift, because there is no cut for the pin to cross.
- ◆ **The customer accepts the free-imagery option.** Critic round 1 caught the film buying paid imagery and then loading free data. The accepted option is now an $85 analysis on free imagery, which matches the real pipeline. Paid tiers stay visible as routing capability.
- ◆ **Persistent "Illustrative scenarios" tag through half 2**, so scenario numbers read as vision and not as customer results.
- ◆ **"Zero humans in the loop" kept as written**, because it is the brief's own copy. Flagged for founders: see below.

## For a founder to check before showing judges

- **"12 agents. Zero humans in the loop."** `NOTES.md` records that paid data orders are bought by a founder by hand and no payment method is connected, and the log tracks real interventions. Judges score real autonomy, so if one asks, the line needs to hold up. To change it, edit `COPY.missionA` in `src/tokens.ts` and re-render. A safer alternative that keeps the rhythm: "12 agents. Every human step logged."
- **Payment and paid-tier imagery** are shown working. `facts.json` tags them as vision, but they appear in the how-it-works half, which has no "vision" tag on screen.
